# Solana to Robinhood Chain

Robinhood Chain is an active destination for supported Solana-origin cross-chain execution. It is not represented here as an active native-origin GASLESS network.

The source wallet chooses an eligible Solana asset and a Robinhood Chain recipient. GASLESS validates a current Relay quote and displays the destination asset plus expected/minimum outcome. Eligible Solana origin network costs are sponsored within policy. Relay coordinates the supported cross-chain execution path, and GASLESS tracks Solana confirmation, Relay/onchain destination state, and final reconciliation.

Current proven examples include SOL, USDC, and USDT on the origin side and ETH or USDG on the destination side. A route can still be unavailable because of registry policy, asset capability, liquidity, amount, recipient, provider health, or current routing conditions.

Credentials, request identifiers, authorization material, polling infrastructure, fixtures, and pilot identities are deliberately outside the public snapshot.
