# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and constructive community. Be considerate, welcome differing experience levels, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and communicate in good faith.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this standard. Reports should use GitHub's private repository contact mechanisms and must not include secrets or unnecessary personal information. Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where practical, and take action proportionate to impact.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
